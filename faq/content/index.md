## Where the answers come from

Most answers were collected from questions asked in the LeviMC community groups between 2023 and 2026, merged, and
checked against the source code, documentation and releases of each project in November 2026. An answer that could
not be confirmed that way says so. Answers that no longer hold for the current versions were left out.

Found a mistake, or a question that is missing? Every page has an **Edit this page** link, and the
[community](https://levimc.org/community/) is where to ask.
